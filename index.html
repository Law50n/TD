THE ARCANE ARCHIVE
📜 Lore Codex

⚔ The Age Before the Shattering

For ten thousand years, the Eight Realms existed in harmony beneath the light of the Spire of Eternity — a crystalline tower grown from the First Spell, cast by the Primordial Wardens at the dawn of creation. The Spire did not merely illuminate; it was the axis upon which all magic turned, the heartbeat of reality itself.

💀 The Coming of the Void Legion

None knew where they came from. Some scholars whispered of a realm beyond the Outer Dark — a place where even light feared to venture. The Void Legion arrived not with war-horns, but with silence. Seven realms fell without a sound. Their essence does not kill; it unmakes. Where they walk, existence simply... stops.

🏰 The Defenders of the Spire

🏹 ARCHER SENTINELS

Blessed by the Moon-Seers of the Third Realm, each arrow is inscribed with a Ward of Return. They never miss; they merely choose their moment.

🔮 MAGE CONDUITS

Scholars who burned their spellbooks to become living channels of arcane force. Their minds house storms. Their eyes see seven seconds into the future.

💣 VOID CANNONS

Forged from the wreckage of the Fallen Realms, these weapons turn the Void Legion's own annihilating force against them. A cruel irony. A necessary one.

👁 The Void Bestiary

🐾 VOID CRAWLERS

The vanguard. Once they were animals of the Outer Dark — creatures of natural shadow. The Legion has hollowed them out and filled them with hunger.

⚡ SHADOW STALKERS

They move between heartbeats. Warriors of the Third Void who traded their mortality for speed. What they lost in flesh, they gained in fury.

🛡 VOID BEHEMOTHS

Mountains given malice. The Legion's siege engines, walking the path that lesser creatures cannot. Even the ground mourns beneath their tread.

👑 RIFT ARCHONS

The commanders. Ancient beings who remember the time before the Void chose them. They wear crowns of broken starlight and carry the weight of a thousand conquered worlds.

⚔ How to Play

🏰 Building Towers

  • Click any empty tile on the path's edge
  • Select a tower type from the sidebar
  • Towers auto-attack by their targeting priority
  • Click a placed tower to retarget, upgrade, or sell

⚔ Combat

  • Enemies follow the glowing path
  • Stop them before they reach the Spire
  • Each enemy that escapes costs 1 Life
  • Killing enemies earns Gold

💰 Upgrades

  • Select a placed tower to see options
  • Up to 2 upgrade levels per tower
  • Sell towers for 60% of total cost
  • Higher upgrades unlock special effects

⌨ Controls

  • P — Pause game
  • M — Mute sounds
  • ESC — Deselect
  • 1/2/3 — Select tower type
  • T — Cycle selected tower's target

🌊 Waves

  • 15 waves of increasing difficulty
  • Press "Next Wave" to start when ready
  • Once a wave finishes spawning, call the next one early for bonus gold — with its stragglers still on the path
  • Beat wave 15 and choose to Continue into the Void — endless, procedurally generated waves for as long as you can hold

🗺 Battlefields

  • Choose your map before each vigil — a tight, mazeable loop or a long, exposed corridor
  • 🗓 Daily Vigil is the same map + modifier for everyone that day — come back tomorrow for a new one

💎 Tower Types

🏹 Archer — Fast, cheap, single target

🔮 Mage — Slow, AoE, armor-piercing, only tower that hits flying enemies

💣 Cannon — Very slow, massive AoE damage

👁 New Threats

🦋 Rift Wisp — flies; only Mage towers can hit it

🔰 Hollow Warden — shielded; break the shield before HP drops

🟣 Void Splitter — divides into two Splitlings on death

🏛 The Sanctum

  • Earn Warden Essence at the end of every run, win or lose
  • Spend it on permanent upgrades — including unlocking the Frost Warden tower
  • Waves 5, 10, and 15 have named bosses with telegraphed abilities — watch for the warning ring before they stomp or heal
  • Click the 🚧 gate near the first bend to seal it for gold — forces future enemies through a longer route

✨ The Spire Endures!

One by one, the silenced lands began to breathe again. The Warden stood alone atop the Spire, armor cracked, staff spent — but standing.

💀 The Spire Has Fallen
Silence claims the last realm.
The Void poured through the shattered gate like water through broken fingers. The Warden fought until the last breath — but even eternity must end. The Spire's light guttered. In its final moment, it sent a single pulse of golden fire across the cosmos — one last memory of what was, transmitted to stars that would bear witness long after all else was forgotten.
